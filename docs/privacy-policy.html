Privacy Policy for GOYE

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GOYE ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account.
  • Course and learning data: We store information about the courses you enroll in, your progress, and your completion status.
  • Device and usage information: We may automatically collect basic technical information (such as device type and app version) to help us maintain and improve the app.

How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide access to courses and track your learning progress
  • To authenticate you securely via Google Sign-In and Firebase
  • To maintain, improve, and troubleshoot the app

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate the app, specifically:

  • Google / Firebase — for authentication and backend data storage

Data Retention

We retain your account and course data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the app's services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us (see below).

Your Choices

  • You can revoke GOYE's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account permissions settings.
  • You can request that we delete your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

GOYE is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
